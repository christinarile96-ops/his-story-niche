### Design A: "Milo Narrator" (closest to the competitor)
> A calm American man in his late twenties with a warm, low baritone voice, telling a personal childhood story to a close friend. Soft, intimate and sincere, with gentle emotional weight and never dramatic or theatrical. Natural conversational rhythm at a slightly fast pace, clear diction, and a subtle smile in the voice on happy lines. Close microphone, dry studio recording, no reverb, no background noise.

Preview text (paste this): *"Every time I left Grandpa's house, he'd stand on the porch and whistle two notes. High, then low. Since I was six, I'd whistle them right back."*

Loudness ≈ 0.5 · Guidance ≈ 0.4 (lower = more natural)

### Design B: "Milo Narrator, warm and husky" (a slightly more emotional take)
> A young adult American male, around twenty-five, with a medium-low, slightly husky and breathy voice. He speaks quickly but softly, like he's remembering something that still gets to him. Earnest, vulnerable and grounded, with understated emotion and no announcer energy. Crisp, close-mic, studio-quality audio with no echo.

Preview text: *"He said, 'Kiddo, I've whistled every single time you left my house. Just quiet enough that you wouldn't hear.'"*

Once you pick one, **save it as a voice and use it for every Short.** A consistent narrator is part of the brand.
