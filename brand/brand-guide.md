# Brand: "Milo Remembers"

> Proposed. Check that the handle is free on YouTube and TikTok before you lock it in.

**Name options (recommended first):**
1. **Milo Remembers** · @MiloRemembers. Every Short is "a memory". The name has the mascot built in and is easy to say in comments ("Milo never misses").
2. **Told By Milo** · @ToldByMilo
3. **Back Then Stories** · @BackThenStories (the backup if Milo is taken)

Why not "Her Story", "His Tale" and similar: that lane is crowded with clones and reads as a copy. A named character builds a fanbase; a pronoun doesn't.

## Mascot: Milo
- Age 10–12 in childhood stories; in "adult" stories he narrates, and we show him older with the same look.
- **Hair:** thick, fluffy **platinum silver-white** hair, messy and wavy, with one big **curled cowlick "wave crest"** sticking up and forward at the front. This is the silhouette people should recognise at thumbnail size, the way the competitor's orange curls are.
- **Eyes:** bright **ice-blue**, and the **left iris has a bold golden-amber wedge** (sectoral heterochromia). It's rarer and more striking than the competitor's two different-coloured eyes, and it isn't a copy.
- **Mark:** a tiny **crescent-moon birthmark** just under the left eye, which also works as a logo detail.
- Light freckles and a gap-tooth smile when he's little.
- **Outfit:** an oversized **mustard-yellow hoodie** with a small navy **"M"** patch on the chest and long hood strings, navy cargo joggers, and white high-tops with **yellow laces**.
- **Family trait:** Grandpa Joe has the **same white cowlick swoosh**, and stories can call back to it.
- Supporting cast reused across stories so fans recognise them:
  - **Grandpa Joe**: white beard, white cowlick, red plaid flannel
  - **Mom**: brown bob, green cardigan
  - **Dad**: stubble, navy work jacket
  - **best friend Theo**: buzz cut, orange tee

Never use orange curly hair, a white tee with a logo, or green pants. Those belong to the competitor.

## Logo
A navy rounded square with a bold yellow **"M"** whose middle dip is drawn as a small speech-bubble tail. The same mark sits on Milo's hoodie and is the profile picture (Milo's face, half-lit, with the M patch visible).

## Captions (house style)
- Font: **Montserrat ExtraBold** (Google Fonts, free for commercial use), sentence case.
- 1–3 words per card, centred at 70% of screen height and about 6% of frame height (bigger than the competitor's ~4%).
- White with a 6px black stroke and a soft drop shadow. **1–2 keywords per sentence** are Milo Yellow.
- Each card pops in (scale 85%→100% over 80ms, ease-out). No glitch effects, no bounce on every word.

## Titles and descriptions
- Title: 3–7 words, first person, ends with **one emoji**, and never spoils the twist. Example: "I Told Grandpa To Stop Whistling 😔".
- Description: one line that teases the story plus 3 hashtags (`#shorts #storytime #animation`).
- No calls to action in the video. The ending loops back to the start.

## Banner / About
- Banner: Milo sitting on a porch step at sunset, with the tagline **"Every memory has a twist."**
- About: "Animated stories you'll want to watch twice. New memory every day."

## Cadence
- Start with 1 Short a day for 2 weeks, then move to 2 a day once the pipeline is smooth. The competitor posts 8 a day, but they have a studio.
